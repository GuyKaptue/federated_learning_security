# E3 research-gap reassessment after the FoolsGold correction (Phase 4A, Phase F)

1. **Was the jitter result caused by an incomplete FoolsGold?** For the all-feature similarity, the evidence says it is driven by that similarity: the +0.29 effect reappears exactly (10/10 seeds, +0.293 [0.141, 0.445]) with legacy and with the corrected-clipping diagnostic arm, and disappears with indicative-feature weighting (+0.009 [−0.007, 0.024] soft). The earlier 5-seed number was a real property of the legacy defence, not of the paper's defence.
2. **Does indicative-feature weighting reduce the effect?** On iid data, yes, substantially (difference vs legacy −0.284, Holm p = 0.014, 10/10 seeds). This is Outcome A for the iid arm.
3. **Does the effect remain after correction?** Not in iid (CI contains 0 and is narrow, upper bound 0.024 soft, 0.085 hard).
4. **Consistent across seeds?** Legacy effect 10/10; soft effect not distinguishable from 0.
5. **Heterogeneity?** Inconclusive (Outcome C): under Dirichlet(0.3) no arm resists the backdoor, and the jitter contrast has a ±0.3 interval.
6. **Consistent with the original paper?** Directionally yes: the paper reports that output-layer weighting defeats noise-based diversification; our result agrees, with the caveat that S_t is our adaptation and the paper's own cost was not examined.
7. **Does the evidence still justify a diversified Sybil experiment?** Not as a "FoolsGold bypass" study. A narrow case remains: (a) the faithful defence pays a large utility price on iid data (−7.7 points clean accuracy, mean benign weight 0.34 vs 0.79); (b) under heterogeneity it did not stop the attack at all. A diversified attacker adds little over these two facts unless it targets the indicative-feature weighting itself, which the paper's adaptive-threshold sybils (Alg. 2) already do in principle.
8. **Untested in the literature (as far as verified):** sources L2 and L5–L9 remain unread, so "unstudied" is not established. Not tested by us: attackers that diversify only in the non-indicative features' complement (i.e. keep output-layer updates similar-looking to benign), larger f, other trigger/models, real clinical data.
9. **Legitimate claims:** (i) a defect in our legacy FoolsGold (−inf → 1.0) and the missing mechanism were found and documented; (ii) the legacy all-feature FoolsGold is evaded by independent noise on iid data in this setup (10 seeds); (iii) an indicative-feature adaptation removes that evasion on iid data at a measurable utility cost; (iv) heterogeneous data is unresolved. **Not claimed:** that FoolsGold is defeated by diversified Sybils, that indicative features universally prevent noise attacks, or that the paper was reproduced.

Outcome classification: **A for iid** (the jitter effect substantially disappears), **C for Dirichlet(0.3)**.
Recommendation: reframe E3 from "can diversified colluders evade FoolsGold" to "what does a similarity-based defence cost, and where does it fail, under heterogeneity"; do not claim a vulnerability based on the legacy code.
