## Cross-cutting finding (affects rows 1, 6, 7, 11)
On iid data (10 seeds, single-head MLP, backdoor ASR), the +0.293 [0.141, 0.445] rise in FoolsGold's ASR when the colluders add jitter is a property of the legacy all-feature implementation. With our indicative-feature weighting it is +0.009 [−0.007, 0.024], a contrast of −0.284 [−0.430, −0.139], and the weighting is the only difference. The +0.29 therefore supports a bypass of the all-feature variant but not of the weighted variant. The weighting is our adaptation (the paper gives no formula) and costs 0.077 clean accuracy (mean benign weight 0.79 → 0.34). Under Dirichlet(0.3) the jitter effect is inconclusive (+0.096 [−0.118, 0.309]) and no variant stops the backdoor, so the finding and its scope are iid-only.


Run counts and timings used below come from stored files: S2-001 has 840 runs (12 defences, 10 seeds), 70 of them FoolsGold, 30 in the affected scenarios (ALIE, MIN-MAX, AGR-tailored, 10 seeds each); S3-001 has 405 runs (11 defences, 5 seeds), 35 FoolsGold, 10 in the affected scenarios (ALIE and AGR, iid, 5 seeds each). Timing basis: FL-E3-BASE 280 runs in 321 s and FL-E3-FGVAL 240 runs in 237 s, both at 15 rounds and 8,000 samples; S2-001 uses 20 rounds and 12,000 samples, so any minute figure is an extrapolation, not a measurement.
