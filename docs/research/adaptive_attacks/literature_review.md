## Consequences for the research design
1. A multi-round attacker already exists (PoisonedFL) and its authors expect cross-silo to be harder. A fair question for this project is whether it, or a simpler history-aware variant, beats the omniscient per-round AGR attacker at K=20, **not** whether the idea is new.
2. RL-based attackers and defences exist (NeurIPS 2022, AAAI 2025). A learned attacker here would be a re-implementation in a different setting, not a new attack class.
3. Heterogeneity weakening defences is established qualitatively (FLPoison, Li et al. 2023, Djemaa et al.). The open part is quantitative evidence on natural clinical-site splits with attackers present, using the small data we have.
4. Nothing in the verified sources addresses robust detection under secure aggregation; this remains open but large and is currently postponed.
