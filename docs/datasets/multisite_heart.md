## Preprocessing
Ten features kept (age, sex, cp, trestbps, chol, fbs, restecg, thalach, exang, oldpeak); slope, ca and thal are dropped (≥ 50 % missing at three of four sites). Target = (num > 0). Cholesterol coded 0 is a missing code. **Protocol A:** each site split 70/30 stratified, clients = the four sites, test = union of site test sets. **Protocol B (leave-one-site-out):** train on three sites, test on all rows of the fourth. Imputation and scaling use training rows only.

## Client partitioning
Site-based partitioning: one client per site (`partition('site', ...)`). Sites differ strongly in prevalence (0.36 to 0.94), which is the point of the benchmark; Switzerland has only 8 negatives.

## Ethical considerations
De-identified research data published by the dataset owners; no re-identification was attempted and none should be. The data are small, old research cohorts and must not be used to draw clinical conclusions. Results here say nothing about deployment in a real hospital network.

## Licensing constraints
CC BY 4.0 (UCI Heart Disease dataset) (verified on the UCI page for the original dataset; mirror licence not verified). Ten features kept (slope, ca, thal dropped: >= 50% missing at three of four sites). Cholesterol coded 0 for every Swiss patient is treated as missing.

## Reproducibility
Everything is seeded (`seed` argument, default 0): the stratified 70/30 split, the partition and the training. `RS_QUICK` is not used here. Re-running `notebooks/03_real_dataset_preprocessing.ipynb` or `load_real(...)` reproduces the arrays bit-identically on the same software stack.

Caveat: n = 920 in total, so confidence intervals are wide.
## Clarifications that apply to every dataset in this project
* **SYN-IDS-v1 is completely synthetic despite its name.** It is generated by scikit-learn `make_classification`; it is not real intrusion-detection data and not patient data.
* **No real hospital data is used and no patient-identifiable data exists anywhere in the project.** The public healthcare tables are de-identified research datasets published by their owners.
* **All federated-learning clients are simulated locally** in one process (virtual clients). The four natural "sites" of the heart-disease data are four 1980s research cohorts, not live hospital federations.
* **Raw source datasets are not redistributed** with the repository. They are downloaded on first use into `data/raw/<dataset>/` and refused if their SHA-256 differs from the pinned value.
* **Some datasets are fetched from public third-party mirrors.** The licence of the original dataset is documented where it could be read on the original source page; the licence of the mirror itself was *not* verified.
* **CIC-IDS-2017 was not available and is not used** in the final implementation. Results on SYN-IDS-v1 must never be compared numerically with CIC-IDS-2017 results.
* **No credentialed clinical repository (e.g. PhysioNet MIMIC-IV, eICU) was available, so none is used** in the paper or in the code. No electronic-health-record data are present anywhere in this project.
